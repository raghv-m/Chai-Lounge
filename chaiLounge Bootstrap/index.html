Indian chai cafe · Edmonton

Real chai, brewed slow on the stove.

Chai Lounge is a small Indian cafe in Edmonton built around one thing done properly: chai the way it is made at home. Strong tea, fresh ginger, cardamom, milk, and time. No syrup shortcuts in the karak, no powders. Just a pot on the stove and a fresh batch every time the last one runs out.

Our story

A chai shop first. A cafe second.

"Chai is not a drink you rush. It is the reason you sat down."

Chai Lounge opened in Edmonton with a short menu and a long memory. Most of the recipes come from family kitchens in India, where chai is made on the stove, argued over, and poured small and often. We kept that habit here. The karak is brewed strong enough to stand a spoon in, the ginger is fresh, and the cardamom goes in whole.

The food followed the tea. Samosas, vada pav, chaat and pav bhaji are what people actually eat with chai, so that is what we serve, next to rice bowls and naan wraps for anyone who shows up hungry. Nothing on the menu is there to fill space.

The room is built for staying. Students camp here with laptops, families take the big table on weekends, and half the neighbourhood seems to drop in after dinner for one last cutting chai. On cold Edmonton nights, that last part matters more than anything else we do.
